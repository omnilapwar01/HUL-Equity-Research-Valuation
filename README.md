# Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) – Equity Research & Valuation

## Project Overview

This project presents an equity research and financial analysis of Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL), one of India's leading Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) companies. The analysis covers the company's business segments, financial performance, working capital efficiency, shareholding pattern, peer comparison, and stock price trends.

The project combines company research with Excel-based financial modelling to demonstrate practical applications of financial analysis, forecasting, and valuation concepts.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze HUL's business model, product portfolio, and competitive strengths.
- Evaluate historical revenue, EBIT, profit after tax, and total assets.
- Assess working capital efficiency and cash conversion cycle trends.
- Examine shareholding patterns and promoter ownership.
- Compare valuation multiples and financial performance with selected industry peers.
- Apply financial modelling and valuation techniques to assess business performance.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Company and Industry Research

### 4. Shareholding Analysis

### 6. Financial Modelling and Valuation
- Historical financial data and forecasting
- Financial ratio analysis
